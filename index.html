CRUZE AUTOMATE

Automation that follows the workflow.

AutoMate is designed to turn repeatable Android tasks into controlled, configurable workflows.

01

Advanced USSD execution

Configurable multi-step carrier workflows with menu observation and trained mappings.

02

Manual execution

Direct user-controlled execution remains available for testing, fallback and operations.

03

M-Pesa-triggered workflows

Configured transaction messages can initiate associated workflows.

04

USSD training

Train keyword and option relationships to handle changing carrier menu positions.

05

Response & action capture

Capture carrier responses after final workflow actions, with SMS confirmation where configured.

06

Retry & fallback controls

Deliberate offer-level retry and fallback behavior instead of uncontrolled repetition.

07

Dynamic step matching

Verify the current carrier menu before selecting the trained option.

08

Tracing & diagnostics

Execution-focused diagnostics help reveal observations, decisions and attempts.

WORKFLOW MODEL

From trigger to verification.

01

Detect

Identify a configured offer, manual action or supported trigger.

02

Prepare

Load configuration and prepare the execution path.

03

Interact

Observe Android and carrier interfaces while the workflow progresses.

04

Verify

Use carrier response and configured SMS confirmation where available.
